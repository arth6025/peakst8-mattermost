# Ad format tiers and what Sloosh renders

Adapted from marketing-skills `ad-creative/references/meta-creative-formats.md` and `static-ad-templates.md` (which re-express Dara Denney's public tier list). The ranking question: does the format open cold, net-new audiences (a scaler) or convert people already mid-funnel (supporting cast)? Both belong in a portfolio. Only the Sloosh column is ours.

## Scalers (reach cold audiences)

| Format | Why it scales | Sloosh route | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder / creator to camera | Persona-native; the person does the targeting | `ugc-video-ad`, `talking-head-video` (real founder clip + script) | medium–high |
| Partnership / creator ads | Starts from the creator's audience | `ugc-video-ad` with the creator's photo; Space `ugc-selfie-video-ad` | high |
| Video sales letter | Education-first categories | `cinematic-product-film` + `talking-head-video` + `captions-subtitles` | high |
| Grid static (many SKUs or one SKU many ways) | Cheap, proven at scale | Space `3-3-product-ad-grid`; `static-ad-variants` | low |
| Authority (expert fronts it) | Trust-gated niches | `talking-head-video` with the expert's own clip; never fabricate an expert | medium |
| David vs Goliath | A clear villain | `comparison-ad`, `static-ad-variants` | low |
| Catalog / product set | Works top of funnel too | `product-imagery` → `platform-content-pack` | low |

## Supporting cast (convert mid-funnel)

| Format | Sloosh route | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Headline statement (one bold line, product hero) | `static-ad-variants` with GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst | low |
| Us vs them | Space `us-vs-them-comparison` via `comparison-ad` | low |
| Stat callout | `static-ad-variants`; infographic model for charts | low |
| Before / after | Space `before-after-ad` via `before-after-ad` | low |
| Testimonial / review card | Space `review-card-creatives` via `testimonial-ads` | low |
| Feature callout | `static-ad-variants`; motion version `product-animation` (Feature Callout Orbit) | low–medium |
| Offer / sale banner | Space `sale-offer-banner-set` via `sale-banner-set` | low |
| Product in hand | Space `product-in-hand-ugc` | low |
| Unboxing | Space `ugc-unboxing-clip`, `stop-motion-unpack` | medium |
| Seasonal refresh | `seasonal-creatives` | low |

## Decayed or risky

Fake chat screenshots, invented reviews, celebrity look-alikes, "doctor" avatars with no real doctor, press-logo walls without the press. Sloosh can render them; the skills refuse them. Use real sources or a different format.

## Hook families (one per concept, rotate them)

Curiosity gap · bold specific claim · first-person confession · contrast / before-after · POV relatability · the buyer's exact question · countdown or challenge. A hook is three parts in video (visual action, spoken line, caption) and two in a static (visual, headline); the parts must not say the same thing.

## Awareness stage picks the treatment

| Stage | Treatment | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | feeling, conversation | learn the situation |
| Problem-aware | pain + outcome, time-anchored friction | see how |
| Solution-aware | state change, documented statistic | compare, demo |
| Product-aware | statistic, objection + proof | try, buy |
