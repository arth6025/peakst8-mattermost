# Festival and season guide

Motifs, light and palette per occasion, with what to avoid. Use the brand palette for backgrounds and the festival palette for props and light. Confirm symbols with the user when the brand serves a specific community; this is a starting point, not cultural authority.

| Occasion | Motifs and props | Light | Palette for props | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diwali | clay diyas, marigold garlands, rangoli, brass trays, sparklers, gift boxes | warm candle light, bokeh fairy lights, dusk | saffron, gold, deep indigo, magenta | fireworks over the product, religious icons unless the brand uses them, overcrowded sets |
| Holi | gulal powder clouds, water splashes, white kurtas, pichkari | bright daylight, backlit powder | pink, yellow, green, turquoise | powder covering the label, muddy mixed colours |
| Raksha Bandhan | rakhi threads, sweets, thali, sibling hands | soft indoor daylight | red, gold, cream | staged family stock poses |
| Eid | crescent and star lanterns, dates, arches, embroidered textiles | cool blue dusk to warm lantern | emerald, navy, gold, ivory | wrong greeting (use "Eid Mubarak"), alcohol references |
| Christmas | pine, ornaments, ribbon, snow, wrapped gifts, warm wood | warm tungsten, window snow light | forest green, red, gold, cream | Santa with the product unless on brand, glitter everywhere |
| New Year | confetti, clock face, champagne-style sparkle, midnight skyline | cool night, neon accents | black, gold, silver | the year written wrong (verify digits) |
| Valentine's | roses, heart-shaped shadows, handwritten notes, two cups | soft pink window light | blush, red, cream | hearts covering the product |
| Mother's Day | flowers, breakfast tray, handwritten card | morning daylight | pastel, white | generic family stock faces |
| Halloween | pumpkins, cobwebs, candles, fog | low orange and purple light | orange, black, violet | gore; keep it playful; `shop-of-horrors` and `halloween-video-ad` Spaces exist |
| Black Friday / Cyber Monday | bold type, price tags, dark set, spotlight | hard spotlight, high contrast | black, white, one accent | reading as a discount store unless the brand is one |
| Summer | citrus, water drops, linen, sand, pool tiles | hard sun, dappled shade | aqua, lemon, coral, white | melting or sweating products |
| Monsoon | rain on glass, umbrellas, chai, wet leaves | overcast soft light | teal, grey, green | water on electronics |
| Winter | wool, steam, frost on glass, fireplace | warm interior vs cool window | camel, cream, charcoal | snow indoors |
| Back to school | notebooks, desks, lockers, bright primaries | flat bright daylight | yellow, blue, red | childish props for adult products |

## Text in images

- Latin scripts: GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst or Nano Banana Pro, short words, verify with `view_asset`.
- Devanagari, Arabic, Tamil and other scripts: results vary; verify every glyph. If any glyph is wrong, use the English greeting or supply the text as an image reference from the brand's design tool.
- Dates and years: double-check digits before delivery.

## Clip ideas (6 s, `image-to-video`)

Diwali: diya flames flicker, a sparkler enters frame. Christmas: snow drifts past the window, ornament sways. Holi: a slow-motion powder puff behind the product. Summer: condensation runs down the bottle. Monsoon: rain streaks on the glass, product sharp behind.

## Sloosh Spaces that fit

`seasonal-ad-refresh` (Christmas, Diwali, summer in one run), `festival-greeting-and-animation` (greeting image + clip), `shop-of-horrors` and `halloween-video-ad` (Halloween), `sale-offer-banner-set` (offer banners in four sizes), `email-whatsapp-header` (header + square).
