# Storefront creative audit checklist

Score each item 0 (missing), 1 (weak), 2 (adequate), 3 (strong). Note the evidence.

## Homepage

- Hero: one product or one idea, readable headline over the image on mobile, product recognisable at thumbnail size.
- Collection banners: consistent style, headline space respected, current season.
- Social proof block: real reviews with names, star counts, photos.
- Video: a short silent loop above or near the fold.
- Mobile crop: hero subject not cut; text inside the safe area.

## Collection grid

- Tile consistency: same background, same crop, same product scale across SKUs.
- Hover or second image: lifestyle or alternate angle.
- Badges legible, not covering the product.

## Product page gallery (per SKU)

Expected set, in order: main on white, three-quarter angle, back or detail, texture macro, scale or in-hand, lifestyle in use, packaging, size or spec card, 5–8 s loop.

- Count present vs. expected.
- Ratio consistency (all 1:1 or all 4:5).
- Resolution: zoom holds at 2x.
- Label legible in the main image.
- People: real-looking, on brand, consented.
- Video present and short.

## Cross-cutting

- Palette and light match the brand kit.
- Seasonal relevance (current promotion reflected in hero and banners).
- Social sizes available (story, feed, Pinterest) for the top three products.
- UGC presence on PDP or homepage.
