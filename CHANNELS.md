## Creating a channel

1. **Name it by type**, lowercase, hyphens only (no dots or spaces):
   - `team-<name>` — a standing team, e.g. `team-platform`
   - `project-<name>-<year>` — has an end date, e.g. `project-peakst8-festival-2027`
   - `<event>-<city>-<year>` — a race or event, e.g. `ironman70-3-goa-2026`
2. **Fill the header** before inviting anyone:
   `Owner: @handle · what it's for · Channel list: <link to this file>`
3. **Public by default.** Private only for money or personal data — say why in the header.
4. **Add it to this file** in the same week — a row in the right table. A channel not listed here doesn't exist for newcomers.
5. **Archive, don't delete,** when a project or event wraps up, and move its row to *Archived* below.
